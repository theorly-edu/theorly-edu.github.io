What Makes Us Different

Learning by building, not watching

THEORLY isn't a course library. It's a place where students learn by creating something real, with an AI companion beside them the whole way.

Learn by Building

Every Mission is centered on creating something real — a model, a website, an app — because concepts stick when you build them.

Guided by Noelle

An AI companion that asks better questions, offers hints, and helps students reason through problems — without doing the work for them.

Certification That Means Something

Certificates are earned by completing authentic work, not by finishing videos — recognition of skills actually demonstrated.

Small, Focused Groups

Cohorts stay small so every student gets real attention, real feedback, and room to think independently.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Mission?+
A Mission is a structured learning journey built around creating something real — like training your first AI model or building a secure website — rather than watching videos or completing quizzes.
Who is Noelle?+
Noelle™ is THEORLY's AI learning companion. She's not a chatbot that hands out answers — she asks questions, offers hints, and helps students think through problems as they build.
Do students need prior coding experience?+
No prior experience is required. Each Mission starts with foundational concepts and builds progressively as students create their project.
How are certificates earned?+
Certificates are earned by completing authentic work — the project a student actually builds — not by finishing a set of videos.
How are Missions conducted?+
In small, focused cohorts for real attention and mentorship. Missions are delivered on-campus in partnership with schools, or in a hybrid format.
How can my school partner with THEORLY?+
Email hello@theorly.in or use the Partner button in the Schools section. We'll schedule an intro call and talk through what a pilot could look like for your school.
